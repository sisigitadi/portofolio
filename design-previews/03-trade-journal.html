Profile — Infrastructure & Security

Twenty Years Keeping the Systems Online.

From racks and runbooks to SIEM consoles and local LLMs — a working operator's record of uptime, triage, and applied AI.

By the desk of SIGIT ADI IRIANTO · Tangerang, Indonesia

Sigit Adi Irianto is an IT & SecOps specialist with twenty-four years in infrastructure and systems administration. His recent years have been spent where operations meet intelligence: security operations centers, threat triage, and applied AI engineering.

Today he administers Docker-based infrastructure on Ubuntu/WSL for a government web platform, monitored by Wazuh SIEM with a DVWA security sandbox for controlled testing. The day-to-day is runbooks, dashboards, and making sure the stack stays up.

“The interesting problems are at the seam — where operations meets data, and data meets judgment.”

His applied AI work is deliberately practical: multi-variable prompt pipelines, BYOK client-side ML tools where keys never leave the browser, and local LLM workflows on Ollama. No magic claims — measurable pipelines.

On the security side, the record includes Wazuh SIEM threat triage that cut mean time to response by up to 45%, FortiWeb WAF telemetry, NIST incident-response playbooks, and ISO 27001 pre-deployment readiness audits.

He supports teams of 50+ staff across production environments, works asynchronously across timezones, and documents everything — the kind of operator you want when the page comes in at 03:00.

Open to remote roles worldwide. Based in Tangerang, Banten — fluent in EN/ID, wired into WIB (UTC+7).
